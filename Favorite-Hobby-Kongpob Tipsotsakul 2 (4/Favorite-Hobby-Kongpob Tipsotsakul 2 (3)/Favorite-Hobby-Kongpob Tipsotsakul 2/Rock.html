My Obsession: The Power of Classic Rock

Welcome to my dark corner of the internet. Music isn't just a hobby for me—it's an obsession, a way of life, the blood running through my veins. When the world gets too loud, I turn to the raw power of classic rock to drown out the noise.

My Rock Collection

My Musical DNA

I live and breathe the music from the 1970s to the 2000s—the golden era when rock was king, and during 70s the anti war songs are top notch.

Genres That Define Me

  • Classic Rock - The foundation of everything. Raw, powerful, eternal.
  • Hard Rock - Heavy riffs that shake your bones
  • Blues Rock - Soulful, emotional, and real
  • Progressive Rock - Complex, dark, and mesmerizing
  • Pop Rock - When melody meets attitude
